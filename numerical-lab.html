Numerical laboratory: covariance and mean momentum

Change the correlation between two velocity fluctuations and observe its momentum flux. Then hold the channel's total shear fixed and change the specified local mean gradient. These constructed models separate a statistical constraint from a momentum balance. They are not experiments, DNS, or calibrated turbulence closures.

A realizable covariance with either flux sign

Let \(X,Y\) be independent random signs, each equal to \(+1\) or \(-1\) with probability \(1/2\). For a chosen dimensionless correlation \(c\in[-1,1]\), define velocities \(u'=2X\) m/s and \(v'=(cX+\sqrt{1-c^2}Y)\) m/s. Each has zero mean. Averaging over the four sign combinations gives \(R_{11}=4\), \(R_{22}=1\), and \(R_{12}=2c\), in m²/s². Their covariance is positive semidefinite for every permitted slider value because it is constructed as an actual expectation of outer products.

The last two quantities use density \(1.2\) kg/m³ and mean shear \(100\) s⁻¹. A negative covariance gives positive effective shear and positive energy production for this positive gradient. A positive covariance reverses these signs. Covariance realizability alone does not prescribe which sign a particular flow produces.

Split a fixed local shear budget

Use the chapter's channel: half-height \(h=0.02\) m, pressure decrease \(G=10\) Pa/m, density \(\rho=1.2\) kg/m³, and kinematic viscosity \(\nu=1.5\times10^{-5}\) m²/s. Symmetry and mean momentum give total shear \(\tau(h/2)=Gh/2=0.1\) Pa. Choose a local mean gradient and compute the covariance needed by that balance:

\[ R_{12}(h/2)=-\frac{0.1-\rho\nu\,dU/dy}{\rho}. \]

The initial gradient gives \(R_{12}=-0.08183333\) m²/s². A gradient of about \(5555.56\) s⁻¹ would let molecular transport carry the entire fixed shear. Larger gradients require a negative effective turbulent contribution to preserve that same local sum. This algebraic possibility is not a claim that every slider choice belongs to a globally realizable stationary channel flow. A local balance supplies neither a full velocity profile nor all covariance components.
